04 · The gift that keeps giving

The Legacy Fund

Rooted here.
Growing forward.

The Legacy Fund is our endowment program. Original contributions remain invested for future growth, while the income helps fund direct expenditures and grants for McSwain students.

Gifts of $500 or more may honor a person, family, business, or organization and are recognized on the Legacy Fund plaque in the Charles Galatro Gymnasium and Performing Arts Center. Anonymous gifts are welcome.

05 · About the Foundation

Independent. Volunteer-led. Student-first.

We turn community support into educational opportunity.

The McSwain Education Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit made up of parents, grandparents, teachers, and community members. We work closely with McSwain School and the district while remaining an independent organization.

Our commitment is simple: invest community-raised dollars in projects that enhance learning but cannot be supported by the school budget.
